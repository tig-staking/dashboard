About this ranking

Each row is an address, not a person. Exchange and contract balances may belong to many holders.

Pending includes TIG in cooldown and ready to withdraw. The staking contract and burn addresses are excluded to avoid double counting.

Holder labels use the first positive TIG receipt on Base: Fresh under 30 days; Established 30–179 days; Long-term 180–364 days; Diamond 365+ days. They do not prove continuous holding. History unavailable means the first receipt could not be verified.

Known contracts keep their names. Other addresses may also be contracts. Balances come from on-chain checks of addresses found through Blockscout and staking records.
